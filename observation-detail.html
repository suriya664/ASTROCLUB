Andromeda Galaxy (M31)

Observed on Aug 01, 2024 at 11:30 PM

★★★★☆
Seeing Conditions

Usually M31 is just a smudge in my backyard, but tonight at the Dark Sky Park it was spectacular. I could clearly trace the main dust lanes extending from the core. M32 and M110 were also easily visible in the same field of view with the 25mm eyepiece.

Used averted vision to see the full extent of the spiral arms. It filled almost the entire field of view.
