Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a telescope to join?

Not at all! Many members start with binoculars or just their eyes. We also have loaner equipment available.

Where are the observation sites?

We have a private site 45 mins from the city center. Directions are provided upon membership registration.

Are kids allowed at events?

Yes! We are family-friendly. However, we ask that young children be supervised around expensive equipment.

How much is membership?

Annual dues are $50 for individuals and $75 for families. This covers site maintenance and event costs.
